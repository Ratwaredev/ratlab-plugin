# Game worlds

Design spaces to reward movement and discovery. Establish landmarks, sightlines, routes, and readable transitions using the project's camera and scale. Make procedural variation preserve navigation and authored composition; inspect generated samples, not just the generator.

World events and NPCs should communicate intent through movement and state changes. Reuse the game's movement, collision, interaction, and inventory rules where they already exist. Keep simulation bounded when entities are far from the player, and ensure returning to a region produces coherent results.
